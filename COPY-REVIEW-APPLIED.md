## Small judgement calls

- **59 Hours** use `7:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.` with an en dash. The report showed a
  hyphen; an en dash is the correct mark for a range. Spacing and a.m./p.m.
  styling match the recommendation.
- **69** "Tuck your pants into your socks" has no trailing period, to stay
  consistent with the rest of the checklist, which uses none.
- **42** The product page heading "Super garlic, not grocery garlic" became
  "A concentrated formulation", since the body no longer says super garlic. The
  same unsupported potency claim on the About page was changed to match.
- **Tree line** — the reviewer's wording uses "tree lines". Applying it left the
  site mixing that with "treelines", so all 18 remaining instances were
  standardised to "tree line(s)".
